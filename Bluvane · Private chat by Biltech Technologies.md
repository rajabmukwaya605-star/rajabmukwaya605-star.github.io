## Say it. Only they hear it.

Pick a conversation, or open People to start a private chat. Everything you send is locked on your device first.

**🔥 Burn timers**Messages that delete themselves.

**⏳ Time capsules**Seal a message until a date you choose.

**⏱️ Study sprints**Type /sprint 25 for a shared countdown.

**🔐 Safety emojis**Verify a friend with a few pictures.

**🫥 Privacy shield**Blurs chats when you look away.

**🚨 Panic lock**Alt+L locks everything instantly.
